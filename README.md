# StyleSphere — Personal Outfit Planner

**Status: Work in progress**

StyleSphere is my personal project for helping people plan outfits from clothes they already own and discover new items that fit their needs and preferences. I originated the idea and am developing it in stages.

## Project Vision

The long-term concept is to let users:

- Add photos of their clothing to a personal digital wardrobe.
- Mix saved clothes into several outfit ideas for each day.
- Plan complete looks with clothing, shoes, and accessories.
- Personalize suggestions by occasion, weather, mood, style, and budget.
- Explore culturally relevant styles, including looks for events such as Nigerian weddings.
- Set optional fit, colour, and other styling preferences.
- Use an AI outfit recommendation engine to suggest personalized looks using their wardrobe and preferences.
- Search for items to buy through product and retailer links.
- Eventually explore features such as reviews and premium options.

## Current Prototype

The current prototype uses HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. It has selectors for occasion, weather, style, and mood, and displays a suggested top, bottom, shoes, and accessories.

Right now, the JavaScript uses fixed rules based on the **weather selection**. The other selectors are not yet connected to the results. The digital wardrobe, saved clothing, shopping features, and AI recommendation engine are planned; they are not implemented in this version.

## Planned Development

1. Connect occasion, style, and mood to outfit suggestions.
2. Add budget preferences and generate multiple complete outfit combinations.
3. Let users add clothing photos to a digital wardrobe.
4. Mix saved wardrobe items into daily outfit suggestions.
5. Build and integrate the AI outfit recommendation engine.
6. Explore culturally relevant and inclusive styling preferences.
7. Add product discovery and retailer links, with reviews and premium options as future possibilities.

## Run the Prototype

Keep `index.html`, `style.css`, and `script.js` together in the same folder, then open `index.html` in a web browser.
